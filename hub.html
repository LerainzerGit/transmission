How a submission works

  1. Tell us about the film

    Add the title, your name, an email address and a short synopsis.

  2. Upload the video

    You get a reference ID, then upload your MP4 or other video file through our upload form.

  3. We release it

    We publish it on the Transmission Releasing YouTube channel. You keep 100% of the royalties.
